Leadership Alignment

Install the operating rhythm that turns strategy into weekly execution—with clear owners, decision rights, and consequences for drift.

Outcome

Cadence, accountability, decision rights.

What It Includes

  • Leadership team charter: priorities, behaviors, and non-negotiables
  • Meeting architecture: purpose, cadence, inputs, and decisions per forum
  • Decision rights matrix (who recommends, decides, informs, executes)
  • Accountability scorecards tied to revenue, margin, and operational KPIs
  • Escalation paths and conflict norms that reduce latency
  • Coaching for executive communication and follow-through discipline

Who It’s For

  • Leadership teams with misaligned priorities or unclear ownership
  • Organizations where meetings consume time without producing decisions
  • Companies post-growth or post-crisis that need a durable operating rhythm
  • Owners transitioning from founder-centric decisions to team accountability

How We Engage

  • Leadership interviews and observation of current cadence
  • Facilitated alignment sessions and operating system design
  • Pilot of new meeting and decision structures
  • Reinforcement over a defined period until habits lock in
