Corporate Turnaround

Focused intervention when performance has eroded—prioritizing cash, margin, operational control, and leadership cohesion.

Outcome

Stabilize operations, restore margin, rebuild leadership alignment.

What It Includes

  • Rapid performance baseline: P&L, cash, backlog, and operational KPIs
  • Constraint identification across revenue mix, cost structure, and throughput
  • Stabilization plan with sequenced actions and clear owners
  • Margin restoration levers: pricing, mix, waste, and cost-to-serve
  • Leadership realignment: roles, decision rights, and crisis cadence
  • 90-day and 180-day roadmaps tied to measurable EBITDA and cash outcomes

Who It’s For

  • Manufacturing, construction-related, and industrial firms under margin pressure
  • Organizations with eroding EBITDA, cash stress, or execution breakdown
  • Boards and owners needing an operator-led path back to control
  • Leadership teams that require external clarity and accountability structure

How We Engage

  • Intensive diagnostic and leadership alignment kickoff
  • Stabilization workstreams with weekly governance
  • Hands-on support through critical inflection points
  • Handoff to sustained operating cadence and metrics
