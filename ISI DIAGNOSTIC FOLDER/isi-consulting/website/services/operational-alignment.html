Operational Alignment & Lean

Align delivery capacity with commercial commitments—removing bottlenecks, improving throughput, and reducing cost-to-serve.

Outcome

Throughput, bottleneck removal, cost-to-serve improvement.

What It Includes

  • Value-stream and bottleneck analysis across production or project flow
  • Capacity vs. commitment modeling so sales and operations share one view
  • Lean-informed process redesign: waste, wait time, and rework reduction
  • Cost-to-serve visibility by customer, job type, or segment
  • Visual management and KPI design for floor and project leaders
  • Cross-functional alignment forums between commercial and operations

Who It’s For

  • Manufacturers and project organizations with chronic late delivery or overtime
  • Firms where margin erodes after the sale due to execution friction
  • Leadership teams ready to connect shop-floor reality to pricing and mix
  • Companies scaling volume without proportional operating system maturity

How We Engage

  • On-site observation and data-driven constraint mapping
  • Prioritized improvement backlog with ROI estimates
  • Pilot implementation on the critical constraint
  • Standard work and cadence to sustain gains
