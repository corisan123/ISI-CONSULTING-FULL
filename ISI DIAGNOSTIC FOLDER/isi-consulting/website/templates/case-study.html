Client profile (example): Mid-market industrial manufacturer · ~$45M revenue · Multi-site operations

Problem

Revenue growth had stalled while gross margin declined 280 bps over 18 months. Pipeline forecasts missed commit by more than 25% for three consecutive quarters. Leadership meetings consumed time without producing clear owners or decisions. Delivery overtime and expedite costs were rising even as backlog quality weakened.

Diagnosis

The Growth & Turnaround Diagnostic identified three binding constraints: (1) qualification leakage—late-stage deals lacked exit criteria and were inflated in forecast; (2) cost-to-serve blind spots on low-margin job types; (3) leadership cadence without decision rights, causing latency between commercial promises and operations capacity.

Actions

  • Installed sales stage exit criteria and CRM hygiene scorecard
  • Segmented jobs by contribution margin and revised pricing/mix rules
  • Redesigned weekly leadership forum with decision rights matrix
  • Aligned capacity model to commercial commit process

Results

  • Forecast accuracy within ±8% within two quarters (example)
  • Gross margin recovery of ~150 bps on targeted segments (example)
  • Expedite cost reduction and clearer backlog quality (example)
  • Leadership meeting time reduced; decision cycle time shortened (example)
