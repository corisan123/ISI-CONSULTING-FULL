What the Diagnostic Is — and Why It Matters

The Growth & Turnaround Diagnostic is ISI's flagship engagement — a structured evaluation of the six dimensions that determine whether a middle-market company can grow, stabilize, or turn around: revenue, margin, operations, team capability, market positioning, and leadership alignment.

It matters because most companies in a performance gap can identify the symptoms — declining close rates, margin compression, operational stalls — but can't locate the root cause. The Diagnostic finds it. Not through a survey. Through a structured, expert-led evaluation that produces a prioritized roadmap and an ROI model, so leadership knows exactly what to fix and in what order.

This is not a vendor assessment. It is not a software tool. It is a senior-led, operator-conducted diagnostic built on the same frameworks ISI uses with clients every day.

How the Diagnostic Works

The Diagnostic follows a structured four-phase process. Each phase produces a specific output that feeds the next.

1

Business Input

You complete the Business Diagnostic Input — a structured questionnaire covering financials, commercial performance, operations, team, and leadership. This gives ISI the raw material to conduct the evaluation.

2

Six-Dimension Evaluation

ISI evaluates the business across revenue performance, margin and cost structure, operational efficiency, team and leadership capability, market positioning, and organizational alignment. Each dimension is scored independently.

3

Root Cause Analysis

Scores are synthesized to identify root causes — not surface symptoms. This is the step most assessments skip. ISI does not. The root cause analysis is what makes the roadmap credible and the ROI model grounded.

4

Prioritized Roadmap + ROI Model

The final deliverable: a prioritized roadmap of interventions ranked by impact and feasibility, paired with an ROI model quantifying the financial upside of each recommendation. Leadership walks away knowing what to do and what it's worth.

What You Get at the End

Prioritized Roadmap

A sequenced list of interventions ranked by impact and feasibility. You know what to tackle first, second, and third — and why that order matters.

ROI Model

A financial model quantifying the revenue and margin upside of each recommendation. Built from your numbers, not from benchmarks.

Executive Dashboard

A one-page summary of the diagnostic findings, scores by dimension, and the top five recommended actions — built for presentation to your board or ownership group.
