Growth Diagnostic Guide

Purpose of this guide

This is the document a prospect receives. It is not a toolkit, not a calculator, and not a substitute for a consultation. It tells you how ISI thinks, which intake to use, and what proof looks like. Working programs, trees, and models are used in the engagement. They are not published here.

1. The constraint is not the complaint

Most files arrive as a complaint: awards are down, cash is always tight, jobs run late, the founder cannot leave the office. Those statements are evidence. They are not a diagnosis. Two companies can say “we need more pipeline” and need opposite work - one has a vacant Business Development seat; the other has a full pipeline of unqualified work the floor cannot absorb.

ISI starts by placing the file in a family. The family sets the questions. The questions set the proof. That is why this site does not use one generic form.

2. Fractional Business Development is the tollgate

If the commercial seat is vacant or ungoverned, more activity will not fix the number. Fractional Business Development leadership is the engagement this practice is built on: a weekly number that is true, qualification used on live bids, and a named owner. Strategy, finance, manufacturing practice, and coaching open only if that seat is not the constraint.

A vacant seat cannot be coached. That file belongs on the Fractional Business Development intake, not the coaching intake.

3. Five families - five questionnaires

Complete the intake that matches the file. After you submit, you will see that family’s client diagnostic glimpse - your answers, framed. You will not see internal engines.

  • Fractional Business Development. Who owns the weekly commercial number. Whether qualification is used on live bids. Win rate. Pipeline that can be governed. Use this when the seat is the problem.
  • Financial. The decision on the table this quarter. Job-level profit visibility. Cash tightness. Coverage pressure. Whether a STOP is allowed to stand. Diligence is first whenever money is involved.
  • Manufacturing / operations. The constraint resource. Where work stalls. OTIF. Quality cost. Whether capex is being proposed before the current resource is exploited. Problem-solving, not a prettier Gantt.
  • Startup and turnaround. One family because both require a fundable sequence and due diligence after intake. Named buyer or recast thesis. Runway in weeks. A dated kill condition. A TAM slide is not demand.
  • Coaching / training. Occupied seats only. Weekly behavior that must change. Proof is a week of notes with owners, run without ISI in the room - not a workshop deck.

4. Diagnostic readiness

A diagnostic is worth running when all of the following are true:

  • A decision-maker will sit in the working sessions, not send a lieutenant to collect slides.
  • You can share facts - financial, commercial, or operational - not only the preferred story.
  • You will allow STOP, CAUTION, or GO, including a stop.
  • If cash, a deal, a raise, or a recapitalization is in this quarter, you accept that diligence comes before a growth thesis.

If those are not true yet, schedule a conversation. Do not force a diagnostic onto a file that cannot use the answer.

5. What a diagnostic covers in the engagement

In the room, ISI tests competing causes, keeps or kills stories, and - when money is involved - runs financial gates before anyone sells a workstream. The 90-day load has owners. Expected outcomes are written as proof, not as a color on a Gantt.

That work uses internal programs and tools. They are not linked from the public site, not included in this guide, and not available to copy from a browser page.

6. What this website will never show

  • Working Monte Carlo, MECE trees, diligence engines, or staff toolboxes
  • Source logic, thresholds, or KEEP / KILL rules
  • Client names, or employer names, without a signed waiver

Public pages are questionnaires, this guide, and a labeled glimpse of how your file will be framed.

7. Sequence after you finish this guide

  1. Read the confidentiality notice.
  2. Open the intake for your family and type facts.
  3. Review that family’s diagnostic glimpse.
  4. Schedule a consultation. Startup and turnaround files should expect diligence in the first working session.
