Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect information you share with ISI Consulting.

ISI Consulting (“we,” “our,” “the firm”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you voluntarily provide through:

  • Intake forms
  • Discovery questionnaires
  • Scheduling forms
  • Contact forms
  • Diagnostic sessions
  • Email communications

2. How We Use Information

We use your information to:

  • Evaluate your business
  • Provide consulting recommendations
  • Build diagnostic outputs
  • Communicate with you
  • Improve our services

3. Data Storage

Information may be stored securely in encrypted systems or session-based storage during diagnostic processing.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell or share your information with third parties unless required by law. See also our Confidentiality Notice.

5. Cookies & Tracking

Our website may use cookies for basic functionality and analytics.

6. Your Rights

You may request deletion or correction of your data at any time by contacting us directly at contact@isiconsults.com or via our Contact page.
