2. Purpose

The Parties wish to exchange confidential information solely to evaluate or perform a consulting engagement, including diagnostic, financial, commercial, and operational information. Optional narrower purpose:

3. Confidential Information

Confidential Information means non-public information disclosed by either Party, including financial data, operational data, customer lists, pricing, forecasts, models, diagnostic outputs, process documentation, and the existence of discussions. Information is confidential whether marked or not if a reasonable person would treat it as confidential.

4. Exclusions

Confidential Information does not include information that is public through no breach, already in the receiving Party’s possession without duty, independently developed, or required to be disclosed by law (with prior notice where legally permitted).

5. Obligations

The receiving Party shall use Confidential Information solely for the Purpose, restrict access to personnel with a need to know, and apply at least the care it uses for its own confidential information (and no less than reasonable care). ISI’s diagnostic engines, models, weights, and practice tools remain ISI intellectual property and trade secrets. Client retains its underlying data. Client receives results of an engagement, not methods.

6. Term

This Agreement continues for months from the Effective Date. Confidentiality obligations survive for three (3) years after disclosure, and indefinitely for trade secrets.

7. Return or destruction

Upon written request, the receiving Party shall return or destroy Confidential Information, except one copy retained for legal or archival purposes under continued confidentiality.

8. No license / no obligation to deal

No license is granted. This Agreement does not require either Party to proceed with an engagement.

9. Remedies

A breach may cause irreparable harm. Either Party may seek injunctive relief in addition to other remedies, without posting a bond to the extent permitted by law.

10. Governing law

The laws of the State of , excluding conflict-of-law rules. Venue in the state or federal courts sitting in that state.

11. Entire agreement

This is the entire agreement on confidentiality. It may be signed in counterparts, including typed signature for a working copy. A paid engagement, if any, is governed by a Statement of Work and Terms of Engagement.
