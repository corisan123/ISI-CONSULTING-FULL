The Unified Growth System for construction & trades

ISI Consulting delivers a single integrated system that stabilizes leadership, reveals root issues, and drives disciplined growth for companies typically between $5M and $50M. This is not traditional consulting and not outsourced business development. It is executive-level leadership supported by a proprietary diagnostic engine.

One system. Three layers. Business Development is the gate.

Fractional Business Development leadership. Diagnostic intelligence. Targeted interventions. The commercial seat first — then only what that seat proves is required.

1. Fractional Business Development leadership

The tollgate. A senior commercial operator inside the company — pipeline governance, qualification, proposal and margin protection, cadence, and coaching for PMs, estimators, and business development staff — without a full-time executive hire. Daniel Reid has owned that seat in construction, architecture, manufacturing, and automation.

2. Diagnostic intelligence

The engine runs in the engagement, not on a public toolkit page. Intake captures facts. Due diligence is first whenever money is involved. Behind the scenes: live financial cases, throughput ROI, uncertainty ranges, MECE issue trees, and decision trees whose logic the client can follow.

Modules activate only when the diagnostic proves they are needed. No more, no less. That is how the offering stays focused and premium.

  • Proposal system overhaul
  • Margin recovery and pricing discipline
  • Leadership cadence reset
  • Training systems for PMs, estimators, and business development staff
  • Alignment between Business Development, operations, and finance
