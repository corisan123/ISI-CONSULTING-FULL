Daniel Reid

Founder & Principal, ISI Consulting. MBA, PMP, LEED AP. Fractional business development leadership is the engagement this firm is built on.

The seat this practice exists to fill

Owners hire ISI when the commercial function is stalling and there is no operator in the chair — or when the person in the chair has never owned a pipeline, a close rate, or a number that had to hit.

Daniel Reid founded ISI Consulting in 2019 after decades of running business development, sales, and project delivery for construction, architecture, manufacturing, and industrial automation. The offer is not a junior bench. It is the same commercial leadership held as National Sales Manager, VP of Business Development, and Director of Sales — installed fractionally, then diagnosed and rebuilt if the constraint is deeper than the Business Development seat.

How ISI works

ISI partners with C-suite and ownership teams to diagnose revenue leakage, rebuild commercial process, and install accountable leadership systems. Customized growth strategy, sales-process redesign, and operational alignment convert an informal commercial function into a structured, measurable engine.

When the firm lacks internal Business Development capacity, Daniel Reid serves as the fractional commercial leader — strategic direction and hands-on execution. Strategy consulting, financial diligence, project management, coaching, and manufacturing practice are in the catalog. They do not precede the Business Development tollgate.
