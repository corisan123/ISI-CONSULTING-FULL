About ISI Consulting

ISI Consulting is built on 30+ years of leadership in manufacturing, construction, business development, and operational transformation.

Mission

Help industrial and project-driven companies convert operational reality into commercial advantage—by diagnosing constraints with rigor, installing accountable systems, and sharpening the leaders who run them.

We exist for organizations that have outgrown informal process and underinvested in the commercial and leadership infrastructure required to protect margin while growing. Our standard is practical excellence: fewer initiatives, clearer owners, measurable outcomes.

Background

ISI Consulting draws on more than three decades of hands-on leadership across manufacturing floors, construction and project environments, business development organizations, and multi-site operational turnarounds. That experience shapes how we engage: we speak the language of operators, P&L owners, and field leaders—not slide decks alone.

Clients engage us when revenue growth stalls despite market opportunity; when EBITDA compresses under cost, mix, or execution issues; or when leadership teams lack a shared operating rhythm. We bring pattern recognition from industrial environments where cycle time, qualification discipline, and decision latency determine whether strategy survives contact with the week.

Our Approach

Diagnostic before prescription

We establish a fact-based view of revenue, margin, operations, capability, market position, and leadership alignment before recommending interventions.

Systems over slogans

Improvement sticks when process, metrics, and accountability are redesigned together—not when motivation is substituted for structure.

Operator partnership

We work alongside leadership teams, installing cadence and capability so the organization owns the outcome after the engagement.

Who We Partner With

Owners, CEOs, COOs, and commercial leaders in manufacturing, AEC, automation, and construction-related businesses who want clarity on what is constraining performance—and a disciplined path to remove it.
