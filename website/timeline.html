From first conversation to load

Client timeline and expected outcomes

This is the partnership sequence. Working engines stay in the engagement. What you see here is the path and the result — not the source code.

  1. Conversation (week 0). Name whether fractional Business Development is the seat — or whether cash, throughput, or a live project is the constraint. Outcome: a go / not-yet, not a catalog sale.
  2. Intake and discovery (days 1–10). Facts, not the preferred story. Forms at /intake and /discovery. Outcome: a fact-base in writing.
  3. Diagnostic glimpse (optional). One public working questionnaire at /diagnostic. It is a peek, not the full suite. Outcome: scored commercial snapshot in the browser.
  4. Gates (days 10–20). Diligence first when money is involved. Competing causes kept or killed. Outcome: STOP, CAUTION, or GO — with numbers.
  5. Matched study (days 20–45). Commercial, cash, CAPEX, manufacturing, quality, project, coaching, or venture — one type, not a blend. Outcome: workstreams, owners, proof of concept.
  6. Quantified path (days 30–60). NPV, throughput ROI, tree, ranges. Outcome: a recommended path and a kill condition.
  7. Load (days 1–90 of implementation). Cadence, owners, unique constraints. Outcome: a weekly number that can be governed — not a slide deck left on the table.
