What Fractional BD Leadership Includes

Fractional BD Leadership is the right engagement when you need senior commercial capability — pipeline governance, proposal discipline, BD team leadership, strategic account development — but aren't ready for or can't justify a full-time VP of BD or Chief Revenue Officer.

ISI embeds a senior BD leader inside your business on a part-time basis (typically 2–3 days per week). They own the pipeline review cadence, coach the BD team, lead key proposal efforts, develop targeting strategy, and build the commercial infrastructure your business needs to scale.

Each 90-day commitment begins with a commercial assessment to establish baseline and objectives. Deliverables are agreed upfront. At the end of each term, leadership gets a full performance summary and recommendation for the next 90 days.
