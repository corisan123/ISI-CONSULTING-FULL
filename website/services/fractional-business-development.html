Fractional Business Development Leadership

Senior commercial leadership, embedded in the business. Built for construction and AEC firms that need an experienced Business Development operator now — without the cost or delay of a full-time executive hire.

Outcome

A commercial engine that can be governed weekly: pipeline, qualification, CRM hygiene, proposal discipline, and a leader who owns the number.

This is the service the website is built on. Strategy, financial diligence, project management, coaching, and manufacturing practice are available. They open after the Business Development seat is real — not instead of it.

What it includes

  • Embedded commercial leadership — typically 2–3 days per week
  • Pipeline architecture and weekly review cadence
  • Qualification standards so “full pipeline” stops meaning “unqualified hope”
  • CRM discipline and proposal / estimate-to-close hygiene
  • Coaching for business development staff, estimators, and PMs who touch the commercial loop
  • Targeting strategy with architects, builders, developers, and industrial accounts

Who it is for

  • GCs, specialty, mechanical, electrical, civil, and fabrication firms where growth stalled and close rates slipped
  • Owners who cannot justify a full-time VP of Business Development but cannot run the pipeline themselves
  • Teams that have demand and still miss awards because qualification, follow-up, or proposal discipline is missing
  • Firms that tried “more activity” and need an operator, not another CRM license

Why this seat, from this operator

Fractional Business Development fails when it is staffed by someone who has never owned a close rate. Daniel Reid has. The proof is the career — named by sector, not by employer.

Mechanical contracting — rebuilt a stalled commercial function

Recruited during declining close rates. Installed the operating system (pipeline, qualification, CRM, weekly cadence) and generated $1.8 million in new project awards within 12 months.

Industrial automation — capital equipment to operators

Generated $48 million in new automation transactions over 24 months, ran a $12 million qualified pipeline, and took ROI cases to C-suite buyers with engineering in the room.

Architectural millwork and construction products — multi-office relationships

Took profit centers from inception to $6.2 million in annual revenue at 67%+ gross margin, and managed 100+ architectural and construction accounts across seven offices.

How the engagement runs

Work starts with intake and a commercial assessment — not a catalog of tools. Objectives and deliverables are named before time is sold. Daniel leads the work himself. Boutique capacity, not a rotating bench.

If the constraint is not the Business Development seat — cash, margin leakage, throughput, or live-project recovery — that is when diligence and the rest of the system open. The tollgate is here so the firm does not buy a diagnosis it will not staff, or a hire it cannot govern.
