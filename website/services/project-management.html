Behind the tollgate

Project management consulting

Estimate, field, and closeout on one set of decision rights. Promises and capacity stay synchronized so claims do not become the operating system.

Outcome

A live job that can be governed: schedule, cost, scope, and recovery named in numbers. Not a prettier Gantt.

Fractional Business Development leadership is still the front door. If sold work already exceeds the constraint, or a job is in recovery, this workstream can open. It does not replace an owner of the weekly commercial number.

What it includes

  • Schedule, cost, and scope on the same decision rights
  • Earned value used in the weekly review, not as a slide
  • Recovery on live work: what to stop, what to staff, what to kill
  • Handoff from estimate to field so buyout and change orders have an owner

What it is not

  • Not software ISI sells or a public scheduling product
  • Not a workshop that expires on Monday
  • Not a substitute for a superintendent or a PM who owns the job
  • Not manufacturing throughput work (that is a separate study)
