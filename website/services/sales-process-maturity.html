What the Sales Maturity Assessment Covers

The Sales Maturity Assessment evaluates your commercial operation across four dimensions: people (capability, structure, accountability), process (pipeline discipline, stage definition, conversion behavior), tools (CRM adoption, forecasting infrastructure, reporting), and pipeline (quality, velocity, health).

Each dimension is scored on a five-level maturity scale. The scoring is honest — we call what we see. The output is a Maturity Score by dimension, a Gap Analysis identifying the highest-leverage improvement areas, and a 60-Day Improvement Plan with specific, prioritized actions.

This engagement is designed to give leadership a clear picture of commercial capability — not a vague sense that things could be better, but a specific, scored baseline they can manage against.
