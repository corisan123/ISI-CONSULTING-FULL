Behind the tollgate

Coaching and training

PMs, estimators, and business development staff. Cadence, qualification, and margin discipline installed as weekly behavior — not a workshop left on the table.

Outcome

One week of notes with owners and a number, run without ISI in the room. A gate used on a live bid. That is proof. A slide deck is not.

What it includes

  • Weekly commercial cadence: who owns the number, what gets decided
  • Qualification used on live work, not posted on a wall
  • Margin in the loop — estimate versus actual, discount authority
  • Seat coaching for the people who touch the commercial cycle

What it is not

  • A vacant seat cannot be coached. That is a hire or Fractional Business Development leadership.
  • Not a public training portal. Materials stay with the engagement.
  • Not a personality workshop. Behavior is measured against the number.

Name the seat first

Coaching follows a real commercial owner. If that owner is missing, start at Fractional Business Development.
