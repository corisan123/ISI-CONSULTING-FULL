Behind the tollgate

Startup and business plan

Customized business-plan development for new or spinning-out construction and AEC ventures. Built from numbers, not a template pack.

Outcome

A named buyer, unit economics, runway, and a 90-day proof. If those cannot be written, the plan is not ready to fund or staff.

This is not the homepage offer. If the parent company still lacks a Business Development seat that can be governed, that seat is first. A plan that assumes a commercial engine the firm does not have is a document, not a venture.

What it includes

  • Who buys, why they buy, and what the first ten jobs look like
  • Unit economics and cash until the proof is real
  • What must be true in 90 days, and what kills the thesis
  • The commercial cadence the new entity will actually run

What it is not

  • Not a generic template, pitch-deck mill, or grant package
  • Not a public calculator or software product
  • Not a substitute for a person who will own the weekly number
  • Not a reason to skip financial diligence when money is in the file
