Behind the tollgate

Decision support, deployed

In the commercial and project cadence of a construction or AEC firm. After the Business Development seat is real. Not a public AI product.

Outcome

A named use in a live review: bid, estimate, or job-cost meeting. An owner, a rule for when the tool is allowed to speak, and a kill condition. A demo that never enters the weekly rhythm is not proof.

Fractional Business Development leadership is still the front door. If the commercial seat is vacant or ungoverned, we install that seat first. Decision support does not replace an owner of the weekly number.

What it includes

  • Where judgment already happens: pipeline review, qualification, estimate vs actual, change orders, recovery
  • What data the firm already trusts, and what it must not invent
  • A narrow deployment inside that cadence, with a human owner
  • A 90-day proof: the tool was used on live work, or it is stopped

What it is not

  • Not a chatbot for the website or the shop
  • Not a generic AI workshop or prompt pack
  • Not software ISI sells or hosts as a product
  • Not a substitute for qualification, earned value, or a person who owns the number

Name the seat first

If the Business Development seat is missing, start there. If that seat is real and the constraint is how decisions get made in the room, this workstream can open.
