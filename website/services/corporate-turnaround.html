Behind the tollgate

Business and financial solutions

Profitability, margin leakage, declining share, and turnaround diligence — construction and AEC. The growth story waits until cash and earnings quality survive a test.

Outcome

A STOP, CAUTION, or GO with formulas — liquidity, coverage, cash cycle, quality of earnings, and NPV — not a narrative that hopes the next award will fix the books.

When this opens

  • A financial decision this quarter: recapitalize, acquire, sell a unit, or fund a turnaround
  • Margin is leaking and nobody can name the job, mix, or discount that is doing it
  • Share is declining and the preferred story is “we need more pipeline”
  • Leadership wants a growth hire before cash quality is proven

What it is not

  • Not a public calculator. Working engines stay in the engagement.
  • Not a substitute for Fractional Business Development when the seat is vacant
  • Not legal, tax, or audit advice. Diligence here is an operator’s gate.

Start with a conversation, not a model

If cash is the constraint, diligence is first. If the Business Development seat is the constraint, that is the engagement the site is built on.
