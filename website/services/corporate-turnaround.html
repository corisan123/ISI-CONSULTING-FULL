What Turnaround Advisory Involves

Turnaround Advisory is for companies experiencing one or more of the following: sustained revenue decline, margin compression that's getting worse, operational breakdown at the leadership layer, or a commercial pipeline that has stalled without an obvious explanation.

We begin with a focused diagnostic phase to understand the root causes — not the symptoms — then move immediately into stabilization mode alongside your team. The engagement is structured around weekly leadership cadence, defined accountability, and milestone-based momentum.

This is not a report. This is a working engagement.
