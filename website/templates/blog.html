Title

Why Forecast Accuracy Is an Operating System Problem—Not a CRM Feature

Category: Sales Process Maturity · Reading time: ~6 min (placeholder)

Insight

Most industrial sales organizations treat forecast misses as a tooling or motivation issue. In practice, inaccurate forecasts are usually the visible symptom of missing exit criteria, weak qualification standards, and leadership forums that accept hope as evidence. Software cannot enforce what the operating system does not require.

Framework

A mature sales process rests on four interdependent layers:

  • Definitions — Stages mean the same thing to every seller and manager.
  • Evidence — Advancement requires documented proof, not narrative.
  • Hygiene — CRM fields are complete enough to manage, not merely report.
  • Cadence — Weekly reviews inspect conversion and risk, not slide aesthetics.

When any layer is missing, forecast “accuracy projects” fail within a quarter.

Action

  1. Write exit criteria for each pipeline stage in one page—no more.
  2. Score current opportunities against those criteria; purge or re-stage what fails.
  3. Redesign the weekly pipeline review around exceptions and evidence gaps.
  4. Publish a forecast confidence rule (commit vs. upside) and hold managers to it for 90 days.
