Guide — not a toolkit

Growth Diagnostic Guide

How ISI Consulting names the constraint before anyone buys a catalog of tools. Construction and AEC. Working engines stay in the engagement.

1. The constraint is not the complaint

Leadership usually names a symptom: awards are down, cash is tight, jobs run late, the founder is the bottleneck. Those statements are data. They are not the diagnosis. ISI starts by naming which family the file actually belongs to — then the questions change.

2. Fractional Business Development is the tollgate

If the commercial seat is vacant or ungoverned, more pipeline activity will not fix it. Fractional Business Development leadership is the engagement this practice is built on: a weekly number that is true, qualification that is used, and an owner. Strategy, finance, manufacturing practice, and coaching open only if that seat is not the constraint.

3. Five families — five questionnaires

Intake is not one-size-fits-all. Each family has its own questions because the proof is different.

  • Fractional Business Development. Who owns the weekly commercial number, whether qualification is used on live bids, win rate, pipeline that can be governed.
  • Financial. The decision on the table this quarter, job profit visibility, cash and coverage. Diligence is first whenever money is involved.
  • Manufacturing / operations. The constraint resource, where work stalls, quality cost, whether capex is being proposed before the constraint is exploited.
  • Startup and turnaround. One group because both require a fundable sequence and due diligence after intake — a named buyer or a recast thesis, runway, and a kill condition.
  • Coaching / training. Weekly behavior, not a workshop. A vacant seat cannot be coached; that is a hire or a fractional seat.

4. Readiness checklist

  • A decision-maker will sit in the working sessions.
  • You can share facts, not only the preferred story.
  • You will allow a STOP, CAUTION, or GO — including a stop.
  • If cash or a deal is involved this quarter, you accept that diligence comes before a growth thesis.

5. What a diagnostic covers — and what it does not

In consultation, ISI runs competing causes, financial gates when money is in the file, and a 90-day load with owners. That work is not published on this website. Public pages are questionnaires and a labeled glimpse of how the file will be framed. They are not the internal programs, trees, or models.

6. Next steps

  1. Read the confidentiality notice.
  2. Open the intake that matches your family. Type real facts.
  3. Review the client diagnostic glimpse for that family — your answers, framed. Not the engine.
  4. Schedule a consultation. Startup and turnaround files should expect diligence next.
