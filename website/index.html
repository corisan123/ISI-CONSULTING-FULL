Where firms get stuck

Does your construction or AEC business have one of these problems?

If so, you are in the right place. These are the symptoms owners bring. Toggle to see what ISI actually tests.

  • Revenue is declining and leadership does not agree on the cause.
  • Jobs win, then margin leaks between estimate, field, and invoice.
  • Forecasts miss. Cash is always tighter than the P&L implies.
  • Projects run late. Change orders and claims eat the week.
  • The shop or yard cannot keep up with the bid board.
  • Growth would break estimating, PMs, or the books.
  • Mix, price, volume, or win-rate — which one actually moved.
  • Leakage in estimate vs. buyout vs. field vs. invoice.
  • Cash conversion versus the story the P&L is telling.
  • Schedule, change control, and decision latency on live work.
  • Throughput constraint in shop, yard, or crew — not “busy.”
  • Whether the next hire or CAPEX exploits the constraint or feeds it.
