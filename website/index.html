What We Do

We partner with leadership teams to identify the true constraints on growth and profitability—then install the commercial, operational, and leadership systems required to remove them. Engagements are diagnostic-led, outcome-oriented, and built for operators who need clarity before capital or headcount.

Diagnose

Map revenue leakage, margin compression, process friction, and leadership misalignment with a structured, proprietary framework.

Strengthen

Rebuild the revenue engine, sales process maturity, and go-to-market discipline without defaulting to full-time executive hire.

Scale

Align operations, cost-to-serve, and leadership cadence so performance gains hold under growth pressure.

Who We Serve

ISI Consulting works with industrial and project-driven organizations where complexity, long sales cycles, and operational dependencies make superficial advice expensive. Typical clients include:

  • Manufacturing and fabricated products companies
  • Architecture, engineering, and construction (AEC) firms
  • Automation, controls, and industrial technology providers
  • Construction-related specialty contractors and suppliers
  • Mid-market operators seeking turnaround, growth acceleration, or leadership alignment

Our Proprietary Diagnostic System

The Growth & Turnaround Diagnostic System evaluates revenue, margin, operations, team capability, market positioning, and leadership alignment. Outputs include a prioritized roadmap, ROI model, and executive dashboard—so leadership can act on evidence, not anecdote.
