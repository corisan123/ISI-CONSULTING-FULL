What the Leadership Alignment Engagement Involves

Leadership Alignment is for organizations where the commercial or operational performance gap can't be explained by market conditions, product problems, or individual skill deficits. When the right people are in place and the strategy is sound but execution keeps stalling — the problem is almost always in the leadership layer.

We begin by mapping the actual decision flow inside the organization: who makes which decisions, how accountability is assigned and enforced, where communication breaks down, and how leadership behavior connects to commercial outcomes. We interview key leaders individually, observe cadence meetings, and review any existing governance structures.

The deliverable is an Alignment Map showing the actual state, a Decision Rights Framework clarifying ownership and authority, and an Accountability Protocol defining cadence, escalation, and performance standards.
