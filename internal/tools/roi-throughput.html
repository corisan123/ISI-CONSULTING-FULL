Throughput ROI model

ROI here is not “investment in, percent out.” It compares exploiting the constraint versus buying capacity. Throughput (T) is the rate at which the system generates contribution. Inventory (I) and operating expense (OE) sit beside it. Change any lever; T, ROI, and the recommendation recompute immediately.

T = min(Demand, Constraint cap × OEE) × contribution/unit × periods  |  NP = T − OE  |  ROIexploit = ΔT / cost of exploit
